Game.csv Log Issues - Wikipedia Explore/Exploit Experiment
==========================================================
Date: 2026-04-13
Version: Wikipedia_Exp_13.04
Participants: 9 (IDs: 1,2,3,4,5,10,11,13,16)

Issue 1: Missing task_start event
----------------------------------
The new Game.csv does not contain any "task_start" action rows.
The analysis pipeline (helpers.py > load_trials) requires both
task_start and task_end to define each trial's time window.

Workaround applied: when task_start is missing, the first event
timestamp in the trial is used as t0 instead. This includes the
familiarity_rating event, so trial duration may be slightly
inflated (by a few seconds of rating time).

Recommendation: add a task_start event in Gorilla that fires
when the participant first sees the Wikipedia interface, after
the familiarity rating screen.

Issue 2: Missing answer_snapshot events
----------------------------------------
The old Game.csv contained periodic "answer_snapshot" events that
recorded the answer text length at regular intervals during each
trial. These were used to detect typing bursts and assign typing
activity to specific page visits.

The new Game.csv has no answer_snapshot events at all. As a result,
the typing/paste binary indicator per page visit relies solely on
"paste" events (which do have timestamps). Manual typing cannot be
attributed to specific page visits.

Impact on analysis:
- Participants who primarily paste (P1, P10, P11, P13, P16) are
  unaffected, since paste events carry timestamps.
- Participants who primarily type (P4, P5) will show typing=False
  for all page visits, even though they wrote substantial answers.
- The "Count Typing" switch metric underestimates actual switching
  for typing-dominant participants.

Evidence (final answer length vs total pasted text):
  P1:  final=1345, pasted=1358  (paste-dominant)
  P3:  final=900,  pasted=377   (mixed)
  P4:  final=1035, pasted=0     (type-dominant, UNDETECTED)
  P5:  final=581,  pasted=0     (type-dominant, UNDETECTED)
  P11: final=6185, pasted=6174  (paste-dominant)
  P13: final=599,  pasted=598   (paste-dominant)
  P16: final=854,  pasted=855   (paste-dominant)

Recommendation: add answer_snapshot events in Gorilla, firing every
3-5 seconds during the task, recording current answer text length.
This restores the ability to detect typing bursts and attribute them
to specific page visits.
